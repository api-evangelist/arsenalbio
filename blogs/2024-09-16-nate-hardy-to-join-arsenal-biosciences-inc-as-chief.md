South San Francisco, Calif. – September 16, 2024 – Arsenal Biosciences, Inc. (ArsenalBio), a clinical stage programmable cell therapy company focused on engineering advanced CAR T-cell therapies for solid tumors, today announced that Nate Hardy will join its management team as Chief Financial Officer, effective October 7, 2024.
