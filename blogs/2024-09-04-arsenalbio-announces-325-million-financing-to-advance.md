Oversubscribed Series C welcomes multiple new investors South San Francisco, Calif. – September 4, 2024 – Arsenal Biosciences, Inc. (ArsenalBio), a clinical stage programmable cell therapy company focused on engineering advanced CAR T-cell therapies for solid tumors, today announced the close of an oversubscribed $325 million Series C financing round.
